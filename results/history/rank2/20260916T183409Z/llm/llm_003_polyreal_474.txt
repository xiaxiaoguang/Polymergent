# Hofmeister Series and PNIPAAm LCST

## Background
The Hofmeister series ranks anions based on their ability to "salt out" (stabilize, lower LCST) or "salt in" (destabilize, raise LCST) proteins/polymers in water. For PNIPAAm, kosmotropic (strongly hydrated) anions promote dehydration of the polymer chain, **lowering** the LCST, while chaotropic (weakly hydrated) anions can bind to the polymer and increase its hydration, **raising** the LCST.

The typical anion order (kosmotropic → chaotropic) is:

$$SO_4^{2-} > Cl^- > NO_3^- > SCN^-$$

## Effect on LCST
- **SO₄²⁻** (strongly kosmotropic): most effective at salting-out → **lowest LCST**
- **Cl⁻**: moderately kosmotropic → lowers LCST somewhat
- **NO₃⁻**: weakly chaotropic → LCST close to or slightly above pure water
- **SCN⁻** (strongly chaotropic): binds to polymer, increases solubility → **highest LCST**

## Ranking (Low LCST → High LCST)

$$\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4 < \text{NaCl} < \text{NaNO}_3 < \text{NaSCN}$$
