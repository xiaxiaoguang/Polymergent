Based on their function and working principle, these membranes belong to the category of ion exchange membranes, more specifically, cation exchange membranes. To achieve efficient proton conduction, this polymer material must possess specific chemical structural characteristics, namely, it must contain a large number of acidic functional groups, such as sulfonic acid groups ($-SO_3H$) or phosphoric acid groups, on its polymer backbone or side chains. These acidic groups can ionize to release mobile protons in a hydrated environment, which are then transferred within the membrane under the influence of an electric field through the 'Grotthuss mechanism' or 'vehicle mechanism', thereby imparting good proton conductivity to the membrane.
