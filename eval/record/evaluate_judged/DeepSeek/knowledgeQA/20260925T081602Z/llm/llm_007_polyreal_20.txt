For example, the conditions of the polymerization reaction should not cause the functional components to decompose.

2. **Introduction and Selection of Spacers**: Typically, the functional components are not directly connected to the polymerizable groups but are linked through a flexible chemical structure known as a 'spacer' (such as a methylene chain). The role of the spacer is to decouple the motion of the functional components from the polymer main chain, providing the functional components with greater mobility to fully exert their functions. It also helps to avoid excessive steric hindrance effects from bulky functional components on the polymerization reaction.

3. **Reversible Protection of Important Functional Groups**: If the functional components contain reactive functional groups (such as -OH, -NH₂, -COOH) that are unstable or interfere with the polymerization under reaction conditions, they need to be chemically protected before polymerization, temporarily converting them into stable groups.
