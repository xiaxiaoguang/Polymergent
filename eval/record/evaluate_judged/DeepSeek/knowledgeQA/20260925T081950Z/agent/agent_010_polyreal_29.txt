gold: Using polymer reagents offers the following benefits:

1. **From the perspective of operational safety**:
    * **Reduced toxicity risk**: By covalently bonding the volatile toxic small molecule reagent to a polymer backbone, its vapor pressure drops dramatically to nearly zero, significantly reducing the risk of operators inhaling toxic gases.
    * **Increased chemical stability**: The confinement effect of the polymer backbone typically enhances the chemical stability of unstable functional groups (for example, small molecule peroxides are explosive, but their stability increases when immobilized). For unstable acylating agents, immobilization can reduce decomposition during use and storage, enhancing safety.

2. **From the perspective of reaction process**:
    * **Simplified separation**: Since polymer reagents are solid, they can be separated from liquid-phase products by simple filtration after the reaction, eliminating the need for complex separation steps such as extraction and distillation, thus simplifying the process.
    * **Facilitates automation and continuous production**: Solid granular polymer reagents can be packed into reaction columns to achieve continuous production, where reactants flow through the column to complete the conversion. This solid-phase reaction mode also facilitates programmed control and automated synthesis, improving production efficiency.
