2. **Polymeric Transfer Reagents**: The function of these reagents is to transfer a specific chemical group to the substrate molecule in a reaction. For instance, polymer-supported Wittig reagents can convert carbonyl compounds into olefins (achieving methylene transfer), and polymeric acylation reagents can transfer acyl groups to substrates.

3. **Polymeric Chiral Reagents**: These reagents contain chiral centers or chiral structures themselves, and their main function is to induce or resolve chiral products in reactions.
