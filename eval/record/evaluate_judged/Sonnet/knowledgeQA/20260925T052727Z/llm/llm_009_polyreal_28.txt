# Three Main Application Types of Polymer Reagents

**1. Polymer Stoichiometric Reagents (高分子化学计量试剂)**
These are polymers bearing reactive functional groups that participate directly in a chemical transformation, being consumed in stoichiometric amounts just like conventional small-molecule reagents. Their key advantage is that after the reaction, the polymer-bound byproduct (or excess reagent) can be simply removed by filtration, greatly simplifying product purification and avoiding contamination of the product with reagent residues.

**2. Polymer Catalysts (高分子催化剂)**
These polymers contain catalytically active groups (or supported metal complexes/enzymes) that accelerate a reaction without being consumed. They combine the high activity/selectivity of homogeneous catalysts with the easy recovery and reusability of heterogeneous catalysts, since the catalyst is insoluble (or easily separable) and can be filtered off and reused for multiple reaction cycles.

**3. Polymer Carrier (Support) Reagents / Scavengers (高分子载体试剂)**
These polymers serve as an insoluble support to anchor, protect, transport, or selectively capture a substrate, reactive intermediate, or impurity (e.g., in solid-phase synthesis, protecting-group chemistry, or scavenging of excess reagents/byproducts). The functional characteristic is that the polymer itself does not undergo the key bond-forming reaction but facilitates separation, purification, or stepwise synthesis by allowing simple physical isolation (filtration/washing) of the polymer-bound species from solution-phase components.
